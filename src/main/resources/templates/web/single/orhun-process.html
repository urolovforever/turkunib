Orhun Exchange Program

The Orhun Exchange Program, operating under the Turkic Universities Union (TURKUNIB), has emerged as the principal higher education mobility mechanism of the Organization of Turkic States (OTS).

Beyond its immediate academic outcomes, the Orhun Exchange Program constitutes a strategic instrument for fostering a cohesive and competitive Turkic Higher Education Area.

Application Guide

Regulation

The goals of these regulations are to determine the basis for the exchange of teachers and students between higher educational institutions that are members of the Turkic Council Turkic Universities Union. This Regulation covers the basics of the exchange of teachers and students between higher education institutions that are members of the Turkic Council Turkic Universities Union.

2026/2027 Orhun Exchange Program Application

The Orhun Exchange Program will commence in the Fall Term of the 2026/2027 Academic Year for students and academic staff of TURKUNIB member universities within the Organization of Turkic States.

Participating countries and universities include Azerbaijan, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Hungary, Uzbekistan, and Turkey.
