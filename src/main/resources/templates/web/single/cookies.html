Cookie Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2025

This website uses cookies to improve your browsing experience, analyze site traffic, and provide personalized content. This Policy explains what cookies are, which types we use, and how you can control them.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your device when you visit it. They enable the site to recognize your device, remember your preferences, and understand how you interact with pages and features.

Types of cookies we use

Type Purpose Examples Retention
Essential Required for core functionality and security (e.g., session management, load balancing). session_id, route Session
Performance Help us understand how visitors use the site so we can improve it. analytics_* 1–24 months
Functional Remember preferences and choices to enhance your experience. lang, theme, consent Up to 12 months
Marketing Used to deliver relevant advertising and measure campaign effectiveness. ad_* 3–24 months

How we use cookies

  • Provide and maintain core site functionality.
  • Analyze traffic, usage patterns, and performance to improve the website.
  • Remember your preferences, such as language or previously selected options.
  • Personalize content and, where applicable, deliver relevant promotions.

Managing cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings and other tools. Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of parts of the website.

Your choices

  • Use “Do Not Track” or similar privacy preferences where available.
  • Opt out of third‑party analytics/ads where such services are used.

Third‑party cookies

Some cookies may be set by third‑party services that appear on our pages (for example, embedded media, maps, or analytics). These third parties have their own privacy and cookie policies. We recommend reviewing their documentation for details on how they process your information.

Changes to this Policy
