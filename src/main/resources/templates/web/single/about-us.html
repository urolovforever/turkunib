About Us

Establishing the Turkic Universities Union (TURKUNIB) in order to give institutional structure to cooperation in the field of higher education among the member states of the Organization of Turkic States (OTS) has been one of the priority agenda items since the founding of the Organization.

Today, TURKUNIB has grown into a wide-ranging program encompassing 170 universities.

The Orhun Student and Academic Staff Exchange Program is also carried out under the framework of TURKUNIB.

Our Members Our History

Mission

To advance higher education in the Turkic world by promoting excellence in teaching and research, enabling credit transfer and academic mobility, and strengthening cultural ties through cooperative programs.

Vision

A vibrant, integrated academic space where students and scholars seamlessly collaborate across borders, driving impactful research and nurturing the next generation of leaders for the Turkic world.

Our Goals

We focus on practical collaboration that benefits students, faculty, and institutions.

Academic Mobility

Student and staff exchange programs; mutual recognition of credits and diplomas.

Common Standards

Developing a shared credit transfer and quality assurance framework.

Cultural Integration

Programs that celebrate common history, culture, and languages.

Faculty Development

Joint training, workshops, and collaborative teaching initiatives.

Competitions & Symposia

Academic competitions, symposia, and cultural events among members.

Scholarships

Support for students and researchers focusing on the Turkic world.

Our History

Milestones that shaped the Turkic Universities Union.

  • 10 August 2012 — Cholpon-Ata

    Initial discussions at the Meeting of Ministers of Education laid the groundwork for cooperation.

  • 22–23 August 2012 — Bishkek

    Decisions adopted at the Second Summit of the Cooperation Council of Turkic Speaking States.

  • 28–29 March 2013 — Istanbul

    Directive adopted to establish a Higher Education Area among member universities of the Union.
