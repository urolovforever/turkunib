Student Council

The Student Council is the voice of students across TURKUNIB member universities. It drives academic mobility, cultural projects, leadership development, and collaboration between youth communities of the Turkic world.

Member Universities Directive

Mission & Objectives

Practical initiatives that empower students and connect campuses across the Turkic world.

Academic Mobility

Promote student exchanges and recognition of credits among member universities.

Culture & Identity

Celebrate common history, culture, and languages through student-led initiatives.

Leadership

Develop leadership skills via forums, workshops, and volunteering programs.

Competitions & Events

Organize academic competitions, symposia, and cultural festivals among members.

Standards & Quality

Support shared academic standards and the Orhun credit transfer framework.

Scholarships

Encourage scholarships for students and projects focused on the Turkic world.

Council Structure

A collaborative body representing students from each member university.

Chair & Vice-Chairs

Elected from among member institutions to coordinate agendas and meetings.

Country Representatives

Each member country nominates student representatives to the Council.

Annual Forum

Annual assembly to approve plans, share best practices, and launch initiatives.
