Membership to the Turkic Universities Union (TURKUNIB)

How to become a member of TURKUNIB: membership criteria, the application procedure, and guidance on preparing the application file.

Membership Criteria

A university seeking TURKUNIB membership is expected to meet the following conditions:

  1. Have organized activities consistent with TURKUNIB's aims and objectives,
  2. Have conducted scientific research or scholarly work related to the Turkic world,
  3. Have established concrete and measurable collaborations with universities in OTS Member States,
  4. Possess experience with exchange programs and the institutional capacity to contribute to the Orhun Exchange Program,
  5. Be ranked within the top 20% of universities in the country where the applicant operates.

Application Procedure

Membership applications follow these stages:

  • Candidate universities submit their applications directly to the OTS Secretariat.
  • The OTS Secretariat establishes a commission composed of 5 persons from Member Universities.
  • The commission evaluates each application against the membership criteria.
  • The OTS Secretariat transmits the commission's proposal to the TURKUNIB General Assembly.
  • Union membership is decided by simple majority at a General Assembly meeting.
  • Universities outside OTS Member States may be granted Observer status.
  • Membership is terminated after three consecutive unexcused General Assembly absences.

Guidance for Application File and Rector's Letter

Membership applications are expected to be substantiated.

The Rector's Letter should be accompanied by a comprehensive application file.

The application letter and accompanying documents should be addressed to the OTS Secretariat.
