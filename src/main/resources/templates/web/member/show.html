How to join

Complete and submit the membership application form along with required documentation including accreditation status, institutional profile, and letter of intent.

Our membership committee reviews your application, evaluates eligibility criteria, and may schedule a site visit to assess facilities and programs.

The board makes a final decision on the membership application. Upon approval, your institution will be formally invited to join TURKUNIB as a member university.
